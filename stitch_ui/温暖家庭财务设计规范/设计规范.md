## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a tactile, domestic, and tranquil financial experience tailored for household wealth management and budgeting. Rejecting the sterile, hyper-analytical blues and sharp geometries typical of institutional fintech, this interface evokes the reassurance of an architect-designed home: warm, serene, deeply organized, and humane.

### Visual Ethos & Tone
- **Atmosphere:** Sunlit linen, matte ceramics, architectural paper, and warm timber.
- **Emotion:** Psychological safety, domestic harmony, transparency, and deliberate calm during sensitive financial decisions.
- **Design Movement:** Modern warm minimalism paired with subtle tactile depth. Crisp grid discipline is softened through generous padding, layered warm-tinted card surfaces, and artisanal terracotta accents.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly on warm-tinted foundations to preserve domestic intimacy and reduce financial anxiety. 

### Semantic Roles & Palette Tokens
- **Backgrounds:**
  - Canvas Base: `#FAF9F5` (Warm Cream Rice Paper) — Global page canvas.
  - Surface Card: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Chalk White) — Elevated card surfaces.
  - Surface Subdued: `#F3F1EC` (Soft Stone) — Neutral interactive containers, input background fills, and secondary badges.
  - Accent Surface: `#FDF3EF` (Tender Terracotta Tint) — Category tags, highlights, and subtle selection states.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:**
  - Text Primary: `#141413` (Soot Charcoal) — Highest-contrast body and headers, warm-tinted black preventing optical fatigue.
  - Text Secondary & Borders: `#B0AEA5` (Warm Greige) — Subtitles, metadata, dividing lines, and default input borders.
- **Brand & Interaction:**
  - Primary Clay: `#D97757` — Primary actions, active navigation anchors, and signature metrics.
  - Clay Hover / Pressed: `#C26343` — High-intent interactive state.
- **Financial Status Semantics:**
  - Positive / Income: `#5B8266` (Sage Herb Green) — Inflows, savings goals hit, surplus metrics.
  - Alert / Overspend: `#D9534F` (Earthy Coral Crimson) — Budget exceedances, subscription surges, mandatory warnings.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes architectural warmth with mathematical precision. 

- **Display & Headings (`epilogue`):** Structural, geometric, and editorial. Provides a reassuring architectural weight to household summaries and statement titles without appearing cold or industrial.
- **Body Text (`manrope`):** Clean, open-aperture sans-serif providing supreme legibility at small sizes for transaction line-items, tooltips, and explanatory guidance.
- **Numerical & Financial Data (`jetbrainsMono`):** Monospaced numeric presentation ensures strict tabular alignment across currency columns, household percentages, and bank reconciliations. All numeric amounts enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout embraces unhurried breathing room to eliminate the cognitive clutter common to ledger apps.

### Rhythm & Structure
- **Base Grid:** An 8pt spatial cadence with 4pt micro-adjustments for inline badges and dense financial rows.
- **Desktop (>=1024px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at `75rem` (1200px) maximum container width to maintain optical comfort. Multi-column balance separates high-level household aggregates on the left (7 cols) from real-time dynamic family ledgers on the right (5 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Sidebars collapse into top summary bars; charts reflow above data lists.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column single-stack layout with unified vertical pacing. Cards span full width between screen safe margins (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids harsh synthetic drop shadows, using soft ambient diffusion tinted with warm umber and terracotta tones to simulate physical stationery resting on a linen surface.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FAF9F5`. No shadow. Baseline ground layer.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Default):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle layered warm shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(176, 174, 165, 0.15);`
  Border: `1px solid rgba(176, 174, 165, 0.25)` to guarantee crisp structural boundaries on high-resolution screens.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Drag Cards):** Slightly lifted state for interactive bills or transaction items:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.04), 0 16px 32px -6px rgba(217, 119, 87, 0.12);`
  Transform: `translateY(-2px);` transition.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sheet Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` elevated over an organic warm scrim `rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.35)` with an expanded ambient bloom:
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, tactile curvature. 

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Enforce a uniform `16px` (`1rem`) corner radius, projecting an approachable, modern ceramic form factor.
- **Inner Containers & Grouped Sub-cards:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) to maintain nested geometric harmony (`Outer Radius = Inner Radius + Padding`).
- **Pills & Status Tags:** Full rounded capsules (`9999px`) for recurring expense tags, member avatars, and positive/negative trend markers.
- **Action Buttons & Form Controls:** `10px` roundedness to provide tactile purchase and clear target affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#D97757` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `10px`, typography `manrope` weight 600. Hover: `#C26343`. Focus-visible ring: `3px solid #FDF3EF`.
- **Secondary Action:** Background `#F3F1EC`, text `#141413`, border `1px solid #B0AEA5`. Hover: background `#FFFFFF` with darkened border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#D97757`. Hover: background `#FDF3EF`.

### Cards & Household Ledger Containers
- Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `16px`, soft warm ambient shadow, border `1px solid rgba(176, 174, 165, 0.2)`. 
- Padding: `24px` on desktop, `16px` on mobile screens. Includes an optional subtle header separator with `1px solid #F3F1EC`.

### Input Fields & Select Pickers
- Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #B0AEA5`, border-radius `10px`. 
- Text typography `manrope` 15px.
- Focus State: Border color `#D97757`, outline none, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px #FDF3EF`.
- Prefix/Suffix Currency Labels: Tinted `#141413` with `jetbrainsMono` font.

### Status Chips & Category Badges
- **Surplus / Income:** Background `rgba(91, 130, 102, 0.12)`, text `#5B8266`, bold dot indicator.
- **Deficit / Over-budget:** Background `rgba(217, 83, 79, 0.12)`, text `#D9534F`, warning marker.
- **Neutral Household Category:** Background `#F3F1EC`, text `#141413`, subtle `#B0AEA5` border.
- Height: `26px`, padding `0 10px`, border-radius `9999px`.

### Transaction Lists
- Row layout with zero harsh dividers; separation achieved with alternating soft hover states (`#FDF3EF` at 40% opacity) or `1px solid #F3F1EC` bottom rules.
- Left column: Category icon enclosed within a `40px` circular `#F3F1EC` disk.
- Center column: Title (`body-md` bold) with timestamp and member tag (`body-sm` muted `#B0AEA5`).
- Right column: Tabular currency (`amount-table`) colored `#5B8266` for incoming credits and `#141413` for normal debits.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkbox: `20px` square, `5px` radius. Active state `#D97757` with pure white checkmark.
- Switch: Width `44px`, height `24px`, track background `#B0AEA5`, active track `#D97757`. Thumb is pure `#FFFFFF` circle with tactile drop shadow.

### Family Member Allocation Meters
- Custom segmented progress bars with rounded pill ends (`9999px`). Track background `#F3F1EC`, filled proportionally using terracotta (`#D97757`) and sage (`#5B8266`) gradients without glossy overlays.
